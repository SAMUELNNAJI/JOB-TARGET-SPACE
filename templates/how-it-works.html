THE PROCESS

Five steps. One meaningful move.

Whether you're hiring or looking for work, every match moves through the same transparent, human-reviewed process.

01

Register

Employers and professionals create an account and tell us who they are.

02

Build your profile

Professionals build a complete profile; employers submit their recruitment requirements.

03

Vetting & review

Our admin team verifies profiles and confirms they meet our quality standards.

04

Smart matching

AI-assisted matching pairs candidates with relevant opportunities based on skills, experience, and fit.

05

Dashboard delivery

Approved, matched candidates are pushed straight to the employer dashboard — ready to review.

BEHIND THE SCENES

Admin-controlled vetting & matching.

Nothing reaches an employer dashboard without human review. Our admin team checks every profile for accuracy and credibility, then matches vetted professionals to open recruitment requirements based on skills, experience, salary expectations and availability — so every introduction is a genuine fit.
